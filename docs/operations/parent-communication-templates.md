# Parent Communication Templates

## 1) Welcome + Match Confirmation

Subject: Block Buddies mentor match confirmed

- Introduce mentor first name.
- Confirm first session date/time.
- Share observation/safety reminders.
- Share support contact.

## 2) Progress Update (weekly)

Subject: Block Buddies weekly progress update

- Session index completed.
- Do/Explain/Debug status summary.
- Any fallback or support notes.
- Next session plan.

## 3) Reassessment/Rematch Notice

Subject: Update on learner support plan

- Explain observed mismatch in plain language.
- Share reassessment/rematch steps and timing.
- Confirm no penalty to family.

## 4) Incident Follow-up

Subject: Follow-up and next steps

- Facts only.
- Actions taken.
- Next safety checkpoint.
- Coordinator direct contact.
