# Incident Drill Protocol

## Drill Objective

Rehearse safety and escalation procedures before pilot expansion.

## Drill Scenarios

1. Safety concern raised during session.
2. Repeated attendance/no-show pattern.
3. Mentor-learner mismatch escalation.

## Response Flow

1. Pause session and document facts.
2. Notify coordinator immediately.
3. Contact parent/guardian with factual summary.
4. Decide continue/pause/rematch path.
5. Record closure note and prevention action.

## Service-Level Targets

- Initial coordinator response: within 24 hours.
- Parent communication: within 24 hours for active incidents.
- Rematch decision: within 48 hours when required.
