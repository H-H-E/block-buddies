# Contributor Types & Roles 👥

> Different people can help Block Buddies in different ways. Here's how.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Parents & Guardians

The backbone of trust in our network.

### How You Can Help

| Activity | Impact | Time | Skill Level |
|----------|--------|------|-------------|
| **Champion the program** | Spread word to other families | Ongoing, light | None needed |
| **Provide feedback** | Help us improve the experience | After sessions | None needed |
| **Supervise sessions** | Ensure safety, observe learning | During sessions | None needed |
| **Host local groups** | Facilitate in-person meetups | 2-4 hrs/month | Some organizing |
| **Administrative help** | Scheduling, coordination support | Varies | Basic computer |

### Getting Started

1. If your child is a mentee → Focus on feedback and observation
2. If your teen is a mentor → Consider helping with coordination
3. If you have connections → Introduce us to schools, libraries, etc.

### Protocols

- Always work through official channels (not direct contact with other families)
- Share concerns through the reporting system
- Respect the privacy of other participants

## 🎓 Educators & Teachers

Your pedagogy expertise is invaluable.

### How You Can Help

| Activity | Impact | Time | Skill Level |
|----------|--------|------|-------------|
| **Curriculum review** | Ensure materials are sound | 2-5 hrs one-time | Teaching experience |
| **Pedagogy guidance** | Shape how mentors teach | Ongoing consultation | Educational background |
| **Assessment design** | Create better progress measures | 5-10 hrs per tool | Assessment expertise |
| **Training development** | Build mentor training content | 10+ hrs per module | Instructional design |
| **Research partnership** | Help study what works | Varies | Academic connection |

### Areas We Need Expert Input

- [ ] Age-appropriate cognitive load
- [ ] Scaffolding techniques teens can actually use
- [ ] Differentiated instruction approaches
- [ ] Learning disability accommodations
- [ ] Motivation and engagement strategies

### Protocols

- Changes to pedagogy require discussion with core team
- Research involving participants needs IRB approval
- Maintain "Cool Result First" philosophy

## 💻 Tech Volunteers

Whether you code or just understand tech, we need you.

### How You Can Help

| Activity | Impact | Time | Skill Level |
|----------|--------|------|-------------|
| **Technical writing** | Better troubleshooting guides | 2-4 hrs per guide | Minecraft familiarity |
| **Curriculum testing** | Validate technical procedures | 1-2 hrs per module | Can follow instructions |
| **Tool building** | Create program infrastructure | Varies | Development skills |
| **Server administration** | Manage practice/demo servers | Ongoing, light | Server experience |
| **Video creation** | Tutorial and training videos | 5-10 hrs per video | Some video skills |

### Technical Areas Needing Help

**Documentation:**
- [ ] Troubleshooting flowcharts for common issues
- [ ] Platform-specific setup guides (Windows/Mac/Linux)
- [ ] Mod compatibility matrices

**Infrastructure:**
- [ ] Session scheduling system
- [ ] Progress tracking tools
- [ ] Resource library management

**Content:**
- [ ] Lesson demo videos
- [ ] Mentor training materials
- [ ] Parent orientation content

### Protocols

- Test all technical instructions on fresh systems
- Document version numbers and system requirements
- Flag when content becomes outdated
- Avoid over-engineering (remember our mentors are teens)

## 🏢 Organizations & Partners

Libraries, schools, clubs, companies—institutional support matters.

### Partnership Types

**📚 Libraries**
- Provide venues for in-person learning
- Computer access for families without resources
- Built-in credibility and trust
- Community announcement channels

**🏫 Schools**
- Participant recruitment pipeline
- After-school program integration
- Credit/recognition for teen mentors
- Curriculum alignment support

**⛺ Youth Organizations**
- Scouts, Boys & Girls Club, YMCA, etc.
- Existing youth program infrastructure
- Merit badge/achievement tie-ins
- Volunteer hour verification

**🏢 Tech Companies**
- Volunteer developer time
- Infrastructure sponsorship
- Mentorship from professionals
- Credibility through association

### Partnership Process

1. **Initial conversation**: Understand mutual interests
2. **Pilot proposal**: Small-scale test of partnership
3. **Evaluation**: Did it work for both sides?
4. **Formalization**: MOUs, agreements as needed
5. **Ongoing relationship**: Regular check-ins

### What We Ask of Partners

- Alignment with our mission and values
- Commitment to child safety standards
- Clear communication and reasonable expectations
- Respect for our grassroots, volunteer nature

## 🤖 AI Agents & Assistants

Yes, really. AI can help—with appropriate guardrails.

### Appropriate AI Contributions

**✅ Green Light (Do freely)**

| Task | Example | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| Documentation cleanup | Fix formatting, typos, consistency | Low risk, high value |
| Content organization | Restructure docs for clarity | Suggest, don't auto-commit |
| Research synthesis | Summarize relevant pedagogy research | Always cite sources |
| Template generation | Create forms, checklists | Templates only, never with real data |
| Brainstorming support | Generate activity ideas | Human curation required |

**🟡 Yellow Light (Human approval required)**

| Task | Example | Must Do |
|------|---------|---------|
| Curriculum content | Write new lesson plans | Flag as "AI-assisted, needs testing" |
| Training materials | Draft mentor guides | Human review before use |
| Communication templates | Email/message templates | Never auto-send |
| Policy suggestions | Propose safety improvements | Always requires human decision |

**🔴 Red Light (Off limits)**

| Task | Why |
|------|-----|
| Direct contact with participants | Human relationship required |
| Safety incident handling | Requires human judgment |
| Matching mentors/mentees | Too consequential |
| Personal data handling | Privacy concerns |
| Final approval of anything | Authority remains with humans |

### AI Contribution Protocol

```
1. Identify: "This contribution was AI-assisted"
2. Scope: Clearly state what the AI did/didn't do
3. Flag: Mark anything needing human judgment
4. Defer: Accept human overrides gracefully
5. Learn: Incorporate feedback for next time
```

### Quality Signals for AI Output

- Does it follow our "Cool Result First" pedagogy?
- Is it age-appropriate in language and concept?
- Does it respect our safety principles?
- Would a real teen mentor find this useful?
- Can it be tested before deployment?

## 🌍 Community Organizers

People who bring people together.

### How You Can Help

| Activity | Impact | Time | Skill Level |
|----------|--------|------|-------------|
| **Start a local chapter** | Bring Block Buddies to your area | Significant | Organizing experience |
| **Recruit mentors** | Find teens at local high schools | Moderate | Community connections |
| **Connect with institutions** | Libraries, schools, clubs | Varies | Relationship building |
| **Run events** | Showcases, orientations, meetups | Per event | Event planning |
| **Build support networks** | Parent groups, mentor cohorts | Ongoing | Facilitation skills |

### Local Chapter Framework

**Minimum viable chapter:**
- [ ] At least one coordinator (adult)
- [ ] 3+ teen mentors ready to start
- [ ] Partner venue (library, school, etc.)
- [ ] Connection to central Block Buddies resources
- [ ] Local parent/guardian support network

**Nice to have:**
- Additional adult volunteers
- Backup venues
- School partnership
- Local tech company support
- Press/community visibility

### Regional Coordinator Role

For those leading chapters:

| Responsibility | Frequency |
|---------------|-----------|
| Mentor recruitment and vetting | As needed |
| Parent communication | Weekly |
| Session quality spot-checks | 1-2/month per mentor |
| Central team sync | Bi-weekly |
| Incident handling (local) | As needed |
| Community relationship building | Ongoing |

## 🎓 Progression Pathways

### From Mentee to Leader

```
Mentee (9-12) 
    ↓ graduates at 12-13
Junior Helper (12-13)
    ↓ shadows mentors
Teen Mentor (14-17)
    ↓ completes 2+ cohorts
Lead Mentor (16+)
    ↓ trains new mentors
Young Adult Coordinator (18+)
    ↓ runs local chapters
Program Leadership (any age)
```

### From Parent to Coordinator

```
Parent/Guardian
    ↓ child participates
Active Supporter
    ↓ helps with logistics
Session Volunteer
    ↓ provides supervision support
Chapter Helper
    ↓ takes on specific roles
Local Coordinator
    ↓ leads chapter
Regional Leader
```
