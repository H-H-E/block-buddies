# My Animal Farm!

Child builds a small fenced pen, lures and keeps at least two animals (cows/pigs/sheep/chickens), names them, and can explain what their animals need to stay put and be happy.

## Your Steps

1. Hold your wheat up! _(then: Select food)_
2. Walk backwards slowly... _(then: Lure animal)_
3. Close the gate! _(then: Secure pen)_
4. Give them a name! _(then: Name animal)_

## Fun Choices

- Which animals first?
  - Cows
  - Sheep
  - Chickens
  - Pigs
- Where does the pen go?
  - Near your house
  - By the pond
  - Somewhere secret
- First animal's name?
  - A silly name
  - A brave name
  - A name from a favorite show

Show your grown-up what you made today!
