# My Story Show!

Child builds a simple stage or scene (castle, forest clearing, puppet show booth), sets up characters/props, and acts out or tells a short story of their own invention with the mentor playing a supporting role.

## Your Steps

1. Build your stage floor! _(then: Place platform)_
2. Who's your main character? _(then: Place character)_
3. Tell me what happens! _(then: Start story)_
4. The end... or is it? _(then: Finish story)_

- Where does your story happen?

- Who is the hero?

- Happy ending or silly ending?
