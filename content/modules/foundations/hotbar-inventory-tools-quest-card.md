# Pick Your Blocks!

Child can open their inventory or creative menu, pick blocks they like into the hotbar, select between them during play, and use one basic tool for its job (axe on wood, shovel on dirt).

## Your Steps

1. Press this to open your blocks _(then: Open inventory)_
2. Touch/click the block you want _(then: Select block)_
3. Now tap a pocket number _(then: Move to hotbar slot)_
4. Use the axe on wood! _(then: Try correct tool)_

- Which 3 blocks go in your pockets?
