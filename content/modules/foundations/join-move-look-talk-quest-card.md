# Learn to Move!

Child can join the prepared world, walk and jump where they choose, turn their view to look at things, and use push-to-talk or in-game gesture to respond during play.

## Your Steps

1. Push here to walk forward _(then: Move forward)_
2. Tap/slide to look around _(then: Turn view)_
3. Press jump! _(then: Jump once)_
4. Hold this button when you want to talk _(then: Use voice chat)_
