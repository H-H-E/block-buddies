# My Dream House!

Child designs and builds their own small house over one or two sessions - choosing shape, colors, door/window placement, and at least one surprise feature - with the mentor building only supporting parts the child assigns.

## Your Steps

1. Pick your wall color! _(then: Choose blocks)_
2. Stack up for walls! _(then: Place blocks vertically)_
3. Punch a window here! _(then: Leave a gap)_
4. Put your door where you want it! _(then: Place door)_

## Fun Choices

- What shape is your house?
  - Tall tower
  - Wide cottage
  - Secret underground
- Favorite colors for walls?
  - Warm (red/orange)
  - Cool (blue/green)
  - Rainbow mix
- Surprise feature?
  - Secret room
  - Flower garden
  - Balcony

Show your grown-up what you made today!
