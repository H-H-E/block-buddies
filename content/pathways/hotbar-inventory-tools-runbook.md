## Session Flow

| Phase | Min | You do | Child does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reconnect | 3 | Ask about last session's exploring spot; Ask what they'd like MORE of today | Shares a memory or wish |
| Choose goal | 2 | Offer: 'Collect your favorite blocks' or 'Get tools like a real builder' | Picks focus |
| Quick win | 5 | Guide opening the inventory once; celebrate loudly | Opens inventory/creative menu |
| Watch or model | 4 | Demonstrate picking one block into the hotbar; Point out the hotbar row at screen bottom | Watches |
| Predict and try | 8 | Ask 'Which block do you think will look best?' then let them choose 2-3 | Fills hotbar with chosen blocks |
| Build or play together | 12 | Suggest placing blocks from different hotbar slots; Narrate switching slots | Builds a small colorful pillar or patch using 3+ block types |
| Remix | 5 | Offer swapping one block for another they just noticed | Swaps and compares |
| Show and celebrate | 4 | Screenshot the mini-build; Ask which block is their favorite and why | Explains a choice |
| Choose next | 2 | Offer next steps: bigger build, animals, or follow a tiny tutorial | Picks a direction |

## Script Starters

> "Today you get to fill your own tool belt - ready?"
> "Last time you explored. Today you collect!"

**Key phrases:**
- "Open your backpack - that's your inventory!"
- "That's your hotbar - it's like your pockets."
- "You pick. It's your build."

## Shared Roles

- **Child (Collector - chooses all blocks and tools):** primary maker and decision-maker
- **Mentor (Helper - points where menus are, never picks for them):** support only
- **Rotation:** Small groups: each child fills their own hotbar; no shared hotbar decisions

## Choice Points

- **Which 3 blocks go in your pockets?** -> Wool colors / Wood types / Shiny stones
- **What should we make first with them?** -> A tall tower / A flat picnic spot / A mystery shape

## Hint Ladder (one at a time!)

1. "Where do you think your blocks live? Let's peek together."
2. "See the three dots / E button? That opens it."
3. "Tap the block once... now tap the pocket where you want it."

## Common Problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Inventory feels overwhelming | Mentor narrows to one tab/category at a time; search box if touch device |
| Blocks not staying in hotbar | Re-demonstrate drag vs tap-to-slot per input method; check for touch long-press behavior |
| Child wants EVERY block | Celebrate the enthusiasm; suggest a 'shopping trip' back later rather than forcing limits |

## Recovery / Reset

Close and reopen inventory; hotbar contents persist in creative mode. If the world state got messy, mentor restores from the pre-session backup copy of BB Starter Plains.

## Solo Fallback (multiplayer fails)

Same goals work fully solo-guided: mentor narrates over screen-share while the child drives their own single-player world.
