## Session Flow

| Phase | Min | You do | Child does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reconnect | 3 | Ask about their farm or house from previous sessions | Shares |

## Script Starters

> "Today your world becomes a STORY! What kind of place should we build?"
> "Ready to be the director of your own show?"

**Key phrases:**
- "Who lives in this castle?"
- "And then what happened?!"
- "You're the boss of this story."

## Shared Roles

- **Child (Director & Storyteller):** primary maker and decision-maker
- **Mentor (Supporting Actor & Stagehand - plays minor characters ONLY when invited; builds backdrop on request):** support only
- **Rotation:** Small groups: audience roles rotate; each child gets a turn as storyteller while others act assigned parts

## Choice Points

- **Where does your story happen?** -> Castle / Forest clearing / Puppet show booth
- **Who is the hero?** -> A knight / A magical animal / Yourself as a Minecraft character
- **Happy ending or silly ending?** -> Happy / Silly / Both

## Hint Ladder (one at a time!)

1. "What does your hero want more than anything?"
2. "Should something surprising happen? Like what?"
3. "How does it end? You pick!"

## Common Problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Child freezes when asked to tell a story | Mentor starts: 'Once there was a knight who...' and hands over mid-sentence; bounded choices help |
| Props look confusing to others | Ask child to explain - THEIR explanation is the story; no 'correct' look needed |
| Two children want same role in small group | Rotate storyteller turns per scene; co-directors can share narration |

## Recovery / Reset

Creative mode: stage rebuilds instantly from backup copy. Stories are never lost - they live with the child.

## Solo Fallback (multiplayer fails)

Fully works solo-guided: mentor listens/reacts by voice while child performs in their own world.
