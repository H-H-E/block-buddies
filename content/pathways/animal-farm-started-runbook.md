# Mentor Run-Sheet: Start an Animal Farm Together

## Preparation

### Guardian setup
- Standard setup
- Note for mentor if child is nervous about animals approaching

### Mentor preflight
- World set to Peaceful difficulty
- Wheat/seeds/carrots available in mentor helper inventory (child picks favorites)
- Fence blocks pre-staged nearby but NOT placed

BB Starter Plains variant switched to Peaceful survival; grassland area near spawn reserved for pens; starter chest contains wheat, seeds, carrots, fences, name tags.

## Quick Win
Spots their chosen animal

## Session Flow

| Phase | Min | You do | Child does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reconnect | 3 | Ask about their house or last build | Shares |
| Choose goal | 3 | Offer animal choices: cows, pigs, sheep, or chickens | Picks animals |
| Quick win | 5 | Guide finding one animal nearby - celebrate first sighting! | Spots their chosen animal |
| Watch or model | 4 | Demonstrate holding wheat so an cow follows you | Watches |
| Predict and try | 8 | Ask 'What do you think happens when you hold the wheat?' | Lures first animal toward pen area |
| Build together | 12 | Help fence perimeter as child directs placement; Gather more fence blocks as runner | Builds fence pen with gate, lures 2+ animals inside |
| Remix | 5 | Offer name tags OR water trough (dig + water) OR color-choice via sheep | Adds one personal touch |
| Show and celebrate | 4 | Screenshot with child standing inside pen among their animals; Child introduces animals by name | Introduces their farm |
| Choose next | 2 | Record next: more animals? crops? expand? | Chooses |

## Script Starters

> "Ready to get some animals of your own?"
> "Which animals do you want on your farm?"

**Key phrases:**
- "Hold the wheat out - see how it follows you?"
- "Walk slow... it's coming!"
- "What should we name this one?"

## Shared Roles

- **Child (Farmer - decides pen location, size, animal choices, names):** primary maker and decision-maker
- **Mentor (Farmhand - gathers materials, models lure once, helps with gate mechanics):** support only
- **Rotation:** Small groups: each child owns own pen; group may share one large pasture with separate sections

## Choice Points

- **Which animals first?** -> Cows / Sheep / Chickens / Pigs
- **Where does the pen go?** -> Near your house / By the pond / Somewhere secret
- **First animal's name?** -> A silly name / A brave name / A name from a favorite show

## Hint Ladder (one at a time!)

1. "Pick the wheat in your hand first - hold it up like showing them!"
2. "Stand close but walk backward slow..."
3. "See it following? Lead it right to your gate!"

## Common Problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Animal escapes through open gate | Normal learning moment! Ask 'what should we change?' - usually closing gates faster or double-gate design |
| Can't find chosen animal type nearby | Mentor walks WITH child toward biome where they spawn; or switch animal choice happily |
| Child upset when animal won't follow | Check they're holding (not just having) food; re-demo once; remind them animals are shy sometimes |

## Recovery / Reset

Peaceful mode = animals never die from mobs. Escaped animals stay in world; mentor can help re-lure. If pen got griefed by accident (broken fences), rebuild together - it's fast.

## Solo Fallback (multiplayer fails)

Fully works solo-guided: same goals in single-player world with mentor narrating remotely.
