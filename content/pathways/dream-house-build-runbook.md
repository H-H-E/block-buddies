# Mentor Run-Sheet: Build a Dream House Together

**Module:** `dream-house-build` (rev 1, status: needs-review)

**Edition:** Bedrock | **Input:** touch, controller, keyboard-mouse

## Preparation

### Guardian setup
- Standard device setup
- Optional: child can bring a drawing or toy as inspiration

### Mentor preflight
- Flat build plot reserved near spawn in BB Starter Plains
- Hotbar pre-fill NOT done - child picks own blocks
- If session 2 of this module: load saved world and locate child's in-progress house

### World spec (flat-creative)
BB Starter Plains with marked 12x12 build plots near spawn; each child gets one plot.

## Quick Win
Places a door on empty ground

## Session Flow

| Phase | Min | You do | Child does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reconnect | 3 | Ask about any new build ideas since last time | Shares ideas |
| Choose goal | 4 | Ask 'What makes a house YOURS?' Offer bounded choices if stuck: tall tower / wide cottage / secret underground | Describes or picks their dream house style |
| Quick win | 6 | Guide placing the front door first - instant house feeling! | Places a door on empty ground |
| Watch or model | 4 | Demo ONE technique they'll need: stacking walls, or making a window gap | Watches one demo |
| Predict and try | 10 | Ask where walls should go before they place them | Lays out walls and corners |
| Build together | 15 | Follow THEIR design decisions; Ask 'what goes here?' not 'put it here'; Take only assigned helper tasks (e.g., gathering more wool) | Builds walls, roof starts, decorates interior |
| Remix | 5 | Offer one upgrade choice: secret room, garden, balcony... | Adds one surprise feature |
| Show and celebrate | 5 | Screenshot from outside AND inside; Child names the house | Names and tours house |
| Choose next | 3 | Record continuation: finish roof? furnish? garden? | Chooses |

## Script Starters

> "Today you're the architect! What does your dream house look like?"
> "Ready to build something totally YOURS?"

**Key phrases:**
- "It's your house - you decide!"
- "Should I help with that part or is it yours?"
- "What's this room going to be?"

## Shared Roles

- **Child (Architect & Lead Builder):** primary maker and decision-maker
- **Mentor (Construction Helper - builds ONLY what child assigns; gathers materials on request):** support only
- **Rotation:** Small groups (2-3 children): each builds own house on adjacent plots; may trade helper roles

## Choice Points

- **What shape is your house?** -> Tall tower / Wide cottage / Secret underground
- **Favorite colors for walls?** -> Warm (red/orange) / Cool (blue/green) / Rainbow mix
- **Surprise feature?** -> Secret room / Flower garden / Balcony

## Hint Ladder (one at a time!)

1. "Where do you want the door?"
2. "Try standing inside your walls and looking around - what's missing?"
3. "Want me to show you how stairs make a pointy roof? Or we can do flat!"

## Common Problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Roof feels too hard | Mentor offers 3 simple roof styles (flat, triangle stairs, pyramid); child picks and tries |
| House looks 'wrong' to child mid-build | Normalize: builders change plans! Offer to knock down just one wall rather than restart everything |
| Mentor accidentally builds too much | Self-check: pause and ask 'whose house is this?' - return control immediately |

## Recovery / Reset

Creative mode: no damage possible. If build collapses badly, mentor restores plot from world backup copy; child's placed door and chosen blocks list are preserved as the 'plan'.

## Solo Fallback (multiplayer fails)

Fully works solo-guided via screen-share; mentor acts as remote construction helper by voice only.
