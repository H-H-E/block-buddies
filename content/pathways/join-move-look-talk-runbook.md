# Mentor Run-Sheet: Join, Move, Look, and Communicate

**Module:** `join-move-look-talk` (rev 1, status: needs-review)  
**Ages:** 6-8 (target ~7)  
**Duration:** 30-45 min  
**Edition:** Bedrock | **Input:** touch, controller, keyboard-mouse  
**Delivery:** solo_guided, shared_world

## Preparation

### Guardian setup
- Sign the child into Minecraft Bedrock before the session starts
- Confirm audio works (headphones recommended) and the video call is open
- Have the device charged and seated comfortably

### Mentor preflight
- Verify world 'BB Starter Plains' loads with flat grass area and creative mode
- Confirm voice channel: child can hear you and you can hear them
- Check input method from intake form; prepare the matching control card

### World spec (flat-creative)
'BB Starter Plains': Flat world, creative mode, clear weather, spawn on open grass with visible landmarks within ~30 blocks: one large oak tree, a pond, gentle hills.

## Quick Win
Walks forward and jumps once

## Session Flow

| Phase | Min | You do | Child does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reconnect | 3 | Greet by name; Ask what they played or built since last time; If first session: ask what they already know about Minecraft | Answers or shows something |
| Choose today's goal | 2 | Offer two concrete choices: 'Walk around and find something cool' or 'Build a little spot' | Picks one |
| Quick win | 5 | Narrate controls once per action using the control card; Celebrate the first successful move loudly | Walks forward and jumps once |
| Watch or model | 3 | Demonstrate looking around by moving the view slowly; Point out something interesting | Watches |
| Predict and try | 7 | Ask 'What do you think happens if you keep walking that way?'; Let them drive | Explores toward their chosen landmark |
| Play together | 12 | Follow their lead; Name things they find; Ask questions instead of directing | Leads a mini-adventure to a place they pick |
| Remix | 5 | Offer: change how you move (sprint/jump) or go a different direction | Tries one variation |
| Show and celebrate | 4 | Screenshot their favorite spot; Ask them to give it a tour or a name | Names or describes their spot |
| Choose next | 2 | Record what they want next: build? animals? more exploring? | Chooses a next idea |

## Script Starters

> "Hey! Ready to explore your world today?"
> "Before we start, show me your screen - is Minecraft open?"

**Key phrases:**
- "You're driving - tell me where to go!"
- "Nice! What do you see?"
- "Want to try it yourself or should I show you first?"

## Shared Roles

- **Child (Explorer - decides where to go and what to try):** primary maker and decision-maker
- **Mentor (Guide - narrates controls once, then follows):** support only
- **Rotation:** In small groups, alternate who picks the destination each round

## Choice Points

- **Where should we go first?** -> The big tree / The water / The hills
- **How should we travel?** -> Walking / Sprinting / Jumping the whole way

## Hint Ladder (one at a time!)

1. "Which way do you want to go? Let's find the button together."
2. "Look at the left side of your screen - see that circle/arrow?"
3. "Put your finger/thumb right there... now slide up gently."

## Common Problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Child cannot find the joystick/move button | Show the control card for THEIR input method; point on screen share; avoid taking control |
| Motion sensitivity or dizziness | Reduce camera speed in settings, take a look-away break, switch to pointing at landmarks |
| Audio echo or feedback | Guardian mutes game audio OR call audio; mentor lowers volume |

## Recovery / Reset

Return to spawn point (mentor uses /tp or walks back with them). If controls feel scrambled, pause and re-show the control card. No progress lost - exploring has no wrong answers.

## Solo Fallback (multiplayer fails)

If multiplayer fails: child explores the same starter world solo while mentor guides by voice/screen-share. Same goals apply.

## Evidence Options
spoken-description, pointing, demonstration, screenshot

## Safety Notes

- Private world or Realm owned by a coordinator/adult; no public servers
- No mentor-child direct messages outside guardian-visible channels
- Mentor never asks for account credentials; guardian handles sign-in
- Session communication on approved video-call platform only
