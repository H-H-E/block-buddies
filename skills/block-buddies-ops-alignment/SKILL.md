# Block Buddies Ops Alignment

## Overview

Keep operational planning documents consistent with canonical pedagogy and delivery mechanics.

## Workflow

1. Load canonical pedagogy first:
   - `docs/pedagogy/engine-v2.md`
2. Audit ops docs:
   - `docs/mvp.md`
   - `docs/metrics.md`
   - `docs/mentorship-program.md`
   - `docs/resource-hub.md`
   - `CONTRIBUTING.md`
   - `docs/contributor-types.md`
3. Remove policy drift and stale checklist items.
4. Ensure launch criteria and QA loops reference Do/Explain/Debug and personalized pacing.

## Guardrails

- Keep safety obligations explicit.
- Keep checklists operational and measurable.
- Avoid introducing unowned process steps.

## Output Contract

Return:
1. Ops-policy mismatches fixed
2. Updated launch/metric criteria
3. Remaining open decisions requiring product input
